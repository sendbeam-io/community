# SendBeam Community

Help, ideas and deliverability talk for people running email for their sites with
[SendBeam](https://sendbeam.io) — one email account for every site you run.

**→ [Open Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/sendbeam-io/discussions)**

## Where to post

| Category | For |
|---|---|
| 🙏 **Questions** | Something is not working, or you cannot work out how to do it. |
| 📬 **Deliverability** | Mail going to spam, DNS that will not verify, the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules. |
| 🔌 **Developers** | The REST API, webhooks, the n8n node, the WordPress plugin, e-commerce events. |
| 💡 **Feature Requests** | What SendBeam should do next. Upvote the ones you want. |
| 🙌 **Tips and Templates** | Campaigns, templates and automations worth copying. |
| 📣 **Announcements** | What shipped. Also at [sendbeam.io/changelog](https://sendbeam.io/changelog). |

## What does not belong here

Anything about **your account, your plan or your invoice**. Answering those means looking at
your data, so they go to [private support](https://sendbeam.io/contact) — we reply by email,
usually within one working day.

Found a **security vulnerability**? Do not post it. Read the
[disclosure policy](https://sendbeam.io/legal/security) or email `security@sendbeam.io`.
There is safe harbour for good-faith research.

## Never post a secret

These discussions are public and indexed by search engines. Never post an API key, a webhook
secret, a Turnstile secret, or a contact's email address.

Redact keys as `sb_live_xxx` and addresses as `j***@example.com`.

Posted one by accident? **Rotate it in your workspace settings first**, then edit the post —
editing alone is not enough, because the old version may already be cached.

## Support by plan

The Free plan is supported here, in public, by the people who build SendBeam. Paid plans also
get private email support: Starter and above by email, Pro priority, Business same-day.
